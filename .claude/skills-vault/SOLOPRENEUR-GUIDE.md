# Solopreneur Guide

> 10 core skills and 5 application chains for the first 30 days

## Core Skills

### 1. Daily Planning (productivity)
Start every day with `/start` and set your priorities.

### 2. Weekly Review (productivity)
On Fridays, review the week with `/retro`.

### 3. Customer Identification (customer-success)
Build your ideal customer profile and understand their problems.

### 4. Value Proposition (product)
Write a one-sentence value proposition and validate it.

### 5. Landing Page (marketing)
Create a landing page draft with `/launch`.

### 6. Content Strategy (content)
Plan a weekly content calendar.

### 7. SEO Foundation (seo)
Identify target keywords and optimize your pages.

### 8. Email List (email)
Build a subscriber acquisition strategy.

### 9. Pricing (finance)
Build a value-based pricing model.

### 10. Sales Process (sales)
Design a simple sales funnel.

## Application Chains

### Chain 1: From Zero to First Customer (4-8 weeks)
1. Week 1-2: Customer identification + value proposition
2. Week 3-4: Landing page + content
3. Week 5-6: Social media + SEO
4. Week 7-8: Sales + conversion

### Chain 2: Product Launch (7 weeks)
1. Week 1: Market research
2. Week 2-3: Product design
3. Week 4-5: Development
4. Week 6: Testing and feedback
5. Week 7: Launch

### Chain 3: Content-Driven Growth (ongoing)
- Monday: Content planning
- Tuesday-Thursday: Content production
- Friday: Distribution and performance analysis

### Chain 4: Sales Machine (2-week setup)
1. Week 1: CRM setup + sales process design
2. Week 2: Automation + templates
3. Ongoing: Daily sales ritual

### Chain 5: AI-Assisted Productivity (1 week)
1. Day 1-2: Badi setup + configuration
2. Day 3-4: Workflow automation
3. Day 5: Skill chain optimization
